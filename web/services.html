Common questions

Does submitting the form commit me to anything?

No. Submission is only an initial inquiry. It does not create a contract, quote, payment obligation or guaranteed deadline.

What information should I include?

Explain the business problem, who will use the system, the must-have first-version features, your budget range and your preferred timing. Do not include credentials or sensitive data.

Can an existing workflow be automated instead of building a full app?

Yes. A smaller automation or internal tool can be a better first solution when it removes the main repetitive task without unnecessary product complexity.

Will AI make final business decisions automatically?

Not by default. Workflows can include explicit human approval gates for quotes, payments, destructive actions, sensitive data or other high-impact decisions.

Have a process that is too manual?

Describe it in plain language. The first step is to identify the smallest useful version worth building.
